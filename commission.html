bio

Hi, I'm kitty, and I have been working with the equine form for over 25 years.
I produce portraits in conversation with the horse; working in response to each animal and letting their character guide the work.

I offer a range of services from loose observational drawing and traditional equine portraits, to experimental fine art in response to a brief.

services & pricing


live drawing from £75

I visit your horse and produce observational drawings live from the stable or field.

This service typically produces expressive, gestural images or pages of loose sketches. I work in response to the subject and choose medium and style on this basis. Optionally, I can observe schooling sessions for art of your horse in work. This service is priced at £25/hour with a minimum 3 hour session.


photo interpretation custom quote

I turn a special photo into a custom portrait.

I reference a favourite or meaningful photo and interpret it into a custom portrait. This service is charged hourly. The time required depends on the scale, detail and finish you're looking for, so please get in touch for a quote.


original portrait custom quote

I reference one or several photos of your horse to create a new original piece.

Rather than recreate a single image, I develop a new composition while referencing photographs of your horse for likeness. The time required will depend on the scope and complexity of the piece, so please get in touch for a quote.


free rein from £90/day

provide me with a brief or direction, and allow me to freely interpret it.

For work exploring broader ideas and themes, I take an outline or brief and interpret it to produce a singular response piece, or small body of work. This type of work is often more abstract, experimental, and self directed. I am always happy to tailor the commission to suit your individual budget and needs.
