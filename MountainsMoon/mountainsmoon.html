The Mountain's Moon

Welcome to The Mountain's Moon — a fiction project by pond. suspended between sky and dream, where Pegasi drift upon silver winds and ancient starlight lingers in their wings.

Welcome to the world of caligo!

Caligo is a closed species project centered around elegance, mythology, and quiet wonder. Each Pegasi is a singular being, shaped by the skies they were born beneath and the stories they carry through the clouds.

Step lightly, traveler. The air here remembers.
