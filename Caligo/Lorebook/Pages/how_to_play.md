# How to Play

There are various different ways to play this game.

You can play the OC game, where you create your own original characters and draw or write about them as you please.

You can play a collaborative game, where you join up with other users and worldbuild or roleplay together, either following the given lore or your own custom design.

You can play the design collection game, where you participate in contests run by other users to win designs.

You can play the ARPG game, where you follow the rules of a given or user-defined RPG to level up your characters.

You can play the closed species game, where you collect and breed designs made under a given adoption thread.

You _could potentially_ play the pet site game, where you adopt digital pterohippus pets. (does not currently exist)

### FAQ
Q: What is the 'given' lore?  
A: This is just the lore that I (pond) wrote. I avoid using the term 'official', because this project isn't about that-- all pterohippus characters and headcanons are valid :)

I think people often participate in closed species without really knowing why they do so. For me, the attractive thing about closed species is experimenting with designs and giving them away. For others, it's the social activity, entering contests, or the process of collecting. I want everyone to be able to participate in a way that suits them. The crucial thing is that we respect one another's preferences, and know that we have a right to curate our own experience; to block or ignore anyone we strongly dislike.

Q: So is it an open species, or closed?  
A: It's both, or neither, depending on what you want. People often complain about closed species being cliquey or difficult to get into. This structure gives everyone the best of both worlds, and opens up the play structure to serve all interests. If you enjoy collecting 'official' designs and restricted rarity, you can follow an adopts artist and collect their designs. If you prefer a lower bar of entry or the freedom to experiment, you can make your own. If RPGs are your thing you can join a community RPG. If it's team games and competition, you can join or run events.  
Every user is at liberty to play under the rules that suit them, and can form groups around their preferred play styles.
