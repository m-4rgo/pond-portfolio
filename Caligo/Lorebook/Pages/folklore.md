# Folklore
![foot](https://www.chickensmoothie.com/oekaki/image/image.php?id=4368499&size=large&format=auto&rev=1766494104)

There are almost infinite possibilities for folklore and mythology around this species.

All the mythology I have written for them is detailed under the next section of the lorebook, but my text describes only one version of this myth.

In real life, many cultures share overlapping mythology for similar concepts, or may have contradictory folklore for the same creature. Each language may construct the name of a beast in different ways. The options for your own worldbuilding are near endless, and you can draw from any cultural influence, past or present.

The lore you have read so far is written from a scientific angle, almost like an encyclopedic entry for a fictional animal. This opens the door for each player to construct their own fantasy lore around the species, or even build on the scientific lore with 'new discoveries' in their world.

In the lore I've written, pterohippi are extinct, but it's up to each player to decide what happens in their own world. Did science bring them back to life, or have some secluded populations survived in secret?
