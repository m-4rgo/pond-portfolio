If you want domesticated winged horses with huge variations in phenotype (like modern dogs and horses) you can tack the "docilis" modifier on to the end of the species name, (like the "caballus" in Equus ferus caballus or the "domestica" in Columba livia domestica). This lets you get away with big variations without having to make a whole new species for what you want.

## Ridden Disciplines
#### Aerobatic Dressage
This discipline involves the performance of aerobatic maneuvers to high standards. Movements may include barrel rolls, spins, loops, and more. There's a useful glossary of aerobatic movements [here](https://regulatorylibrary.caa.co.uk/2018-1976/Content/CS/Appendix%20F%20Glossary%20of%20aerobatic.htm).  
More resources can be found [here(1)](https://www.iac.org/aerobatic-figures), [here(2)(video)](https://www.skycombatace.com/the-ultimate-guide-to-flying-aerobatic-maneuvers-at-sky-combat-ace), [here(3)](https://www.flyinggiants.com/forums/showthread.php?t=34670), [here(4)](https://www.flitetest.com/articles/essential-aerobatic-maneuvers-for-rc-planes), and [here(5)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerobatic_maneuver).  
Saddle modifications providing additional security for the rider can be made. For example, safety-release knee fastening and flight harnesses for securing the rider to the saddle.
#### Sky Racing
This is speed racing which has various versions: sprints, endurance, obstacle (like a cross-country equivalent), circuit, hoops (involves flying through suspended hoops without incurring penalties), and more.
