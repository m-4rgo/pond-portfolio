STAR POINTS

Introducing: our "self-checkout" art/literature point system! Here at EquusAvis we focus on improving our work; our art, our writing, our stories, our code... And we love making artwork that we are proud of and that pushes us to the best of our ability! This is why you get to truthfully award your own star points based on how you feel about the art you have made; it's all about your own creative journey! You can earn Starpoints for almost anything that's creative and to do with EquusAvis: artwork, poetry, literature, coding (like personal websites or forum posting tables that were made by you and are specific for your character). If you're not sure if you can earn Starpoints for it, ask us!
To earn Starpoints, you have to measure your piece of art against 3 criteria: quality, effort, and satisfaction. It is important to consider: you might make a piece of art that is good quality, but it might not have taken much effort. In this case you should be truthful to yourself! Think about how much better it could have been if you put in more effort! Similarly, you might have put in a lot of effort, but the piece still doesn't satisfy you for some reason. If you pretend you're satisfied and give yourself too many stars for satisfaction, you'll hinder your growth! Each criteria can be awarded 0-3 stars. Think carefully about the number of stars you award :)


QUALITY :star::star::star:

How does this piece look against the other work in your gallery? Is it better or worse than work you have made before? And how does it compare to other artists?
EFFORT :star::star::star:

How long did this piece take to complete? Longer or shorter than usual? Did you a lot of do research to make this piece? Did you use or seek out references to make the piece better?
SATISFACTION :star::star::star:

How happy are you with this piece? Do you like the overall outcome? Think about the composition, the detail. Is it your best?

LEVELS

You can use Starpoints to level-up your character, and to unlock special items!
You can track your character's level in the level tracker which is located on the left hand side of the reference template, here:
Reference by p-ond

There are six levels: redRed Star, orangeOrange Star, yellowYellow Star, greenGreen Star, blueBlue Star, and violetViolet Star. There are three Stars to gain in each level. Each Star is worth 9 Starpoints, so for each 9 Starpoints you earn, you can add one Star stamp to your character's tracker. If you have more than one character, you can choose which character to award your Starpoints to. It may be helpful for you to track your Starpoints, either in your character's reference or in a Starpoint-tracking journal.


CONCOCTIONS aka 'CONCOs'

Sleipnir by EquusAvis-Admin
Pisces by EquusAvis-Admin
Lyrebird by EquusAvis-Admin
Lucent by EquusAvis-Admin

Concoctions, or 'Concos' are magic potions which can cause physical changes to your EQA. Once you have unlocked or purchased a conco, you may use it forever for as many EQA designs with that conco as you like for roleplay in THE WORLD. You may also trade and sell your designs to other users. You must always credit p-ond for the creation of the species when you are making EquusAvis designs!
Your characters' designs should only use concos that you have unlocked!(note: only when roleplaying in the group! all items are free for you to use if you wish to roleplay elsewhere c: ). If you are inspired by our concos and make and sell designs that are not EquusAvis but are of other nondescript equine fantasy breeds, please credit us (EquusAvis or p-ond) for your inspiration (and we would appreciate a donation to help the group run if you'd like to give it <3)!
Some concos can only be unlocked through roleplay or levelling up, and some can be purchased before unlock for immediate use (all profits from these sales feed directly back into the group!).
Visit the BAZAAR to purchase concos!


ROBES: FANCY TACK

Robes are extravagant and fancy garments that EQAs can wear to display status and level. The basic robes are made of precious metals and fine fabrics, however, when you find other items, you can adorn your character's robes with those too! There are six areas you can unlock robes for: head and neck, front legs, hind legs, body core, wings, and tail. Like concos, they can be unlocked, or they can be bought before unlock for immediate use.

OTHER ITEMS

Bag by p-ond

There are other items which can be found, unlocked, and purchased. These include items that can be used to craft armour, tools, weapons, and other fun accessories! The Bag is where you can track which items you own and how many of them you have. You can trade or sell some items to other users. Go to the BAZAAR to find out more about these items.

DAILY DICE

[DAILY DICE]
Daily dice is an app on our discord server where you can find rare items. These items can be used to craft accessories, tools, and weapons, and the recipes can be discovered during guided storylines in THE WORLD.
Your character can also "find" some common items when you make creative work of them doing so. For example, they can find wood and feathers if you write a piece of literature about the scene and circumstances that they find those materials, or perhaps draw an image of the situation.

CRAFTABLES

Using the items you can find from the daily dice and in THE WORLD, you can craft other items like weapons, jewellery, and tools! The recipes for these items can be discovered through guided storylines in THE WORLD. Each time a recipe is found in THE WORLD, it will be listed here for everyone to use!
