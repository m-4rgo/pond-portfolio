## BREED PHYSIOLOGY, CONFORMATION & TRAITS

https://www.deviantart.com/p-ond/art/Annotated-Diagram-758190104  
Annotated Diagram by p-ond  
These visual references may help you!  

- Equus Aves are omnivorous; they hunt bugs and small birds and mammals, as well as fish, but also consume various types of plantae and algae, including grasses, lichen, moss, and bark! They generally hunt animals that are smaller than a pheasant or in some cases, badger.  
- Only EQAs whose sex is female may have fangs. The fangs can be controlled by muscles in the face, which can indicate aggression (fangs forward), submission (fangs back), amongst other emotions! We have a special item that will allow fangs on males, though the fangs do not grow as large as the females’ and are static, meaning that they can’t be controlled by any facial muscles or move.  
- Equus Aves are always herring gutted! This trait makes them more aerodynamic and aids their flight efficiency.  
- EQAs’ nostrils are able to be closed. This protects their head from high pressure when flying and diving.  
- EQAs' eyes are large and are placed more frontal on the face than a horse's eyes. This more frontal placement helps them to be more proficient hunters, and gives them a wider range of vision in which they can perceive depth, which helps with flight and coordination. They often have long eyelashes.  
- Short pasterns help EQAs to make fast take-offs from the ground.  
- There is minimal or no crest muscle on the top of the neck. EQAs with a large crest may find their flight is hindered due to their centre of gravity no longer resting directly below their wing joints (because the weight of the muscle skews their weight distribution to their front end!)  
- EQAs’ ears have thick hair growing from them. This helps reduce wind noise and parasites.  
- EQAs have a thin haired, water resistant coat.  
- EQAs have 'fingers' on their wings, which they can use to grasp, hold, and manipulate objects. They're quite dexterous!  
- EQAs communicate with one another via normal horse vocalisations, but may also use zebra and donkey’s bays, as well as screeches, chirps, and song-like bird whistles.  
- EQA foals are formed inside an egg. The egg, whilst still inside the body has a soft shell, somewhat like a calcified, more reinforced amniotic sac. A foal can be carried to term inside this soft egg within the body, or the egg can be laid before the foal is at full term (at which point its shell hardens) and incubated until it hatches. Eggs can be laid as early as three months. Gestation is between 6 & 10 months.  
- In herds, EQAs usually adopt a matriarchal structure, much like hyenas. Lone females are common; they often mate, incubate an egg for three months, then lay it and leave it with a male to incubate externally. During this time, pseudo-teats which are present on the male will start to lactate. It is not uncommon for lactating mares and stallions (and geldings can lactate too!) to group-parent a generation of foals.  
- EQAs have six toes! They have four on their main hoof, and two toes protruding from their ankle, much like an ergot. This number of toes helps keep them stable on rocky and slippery terrain and helps distribute their weight more evenly on soft terrain like sand (a bit like camels).

## CHARACTER CREATION

EquusAvis is a semi-open species. Standard EQAs are free and open to create! Follow the guidelines below to create your own. If you want to create a character with special features, you will either have to unlock or purchase access to those features. You can do virtually anything with your EQA, so long as it is for non-profit, personal use only. You can join in on THE WORLD, EQAs' open and free world which has a play-at-your-own-pace type storyline if you want to get involved with it. You can plot your own stories in THE WORLD, or you can play your EQAs in any other world or RP that you like! You must ALWAYS! credit the person who designed your EQA, and credit p-ond for the creation of the species.


This is the group's reference template, which you are free to use for your EQA's reference image! We encourage you to draw your own reference, but if you use somebody else's artwork on your reference, please credit the artist in the description.
### BODY & BUILD

Your character can have virtually any colouring that is found on real-life equines (both extant and extinct!). The default & traditional build of an Equus Avis is lightweight, fine, refined, with a slightly concave face (as seen in the visual examples above. The closest real-world example is a herring-gutted akhal-teke with some egyptian-arabian blood). This is the most life-like Pegasus species we could come up with! However, you are free to create a character which deviates from this biologically-ideal default. Here's some options to help you get started!

### BUILD

#### Light ![unlocked](images/unlocked.gif)  Available!

Like Akhal Tekes, Arabians, and Thoroughbreds. 12-16 hands
#### Baroque ![unlocked](images/unlocked.gif)  Available!

Like Friesians, Andalusians, and other Iberian breeds. You can add small feathers to the EQA's legs. 14-16 hands.
#### Wild ![locked](images/locked.gif) You must first unlock this.

Like donkeys, mules, asses, and zebra (and yes, ass and zebra ears are included in this!). 9-17 hands.
#### Draft ![locked](images/locked.gif) You must first unlock this.

Any type of cold blood horse from the Vanner to the Ardennais. You can use copious amounts of feathering! 14-21 hands.
#### Pygmy ![locked](images/locked.gif) You must first unlock this.

Like a refined miniature horse! 5-7 hands.
#### Goliath ![locked](images/locked.gif) You must first unlock this.

Gargantuan EQAs that tower above all others! 25-40 hands.

#### TAIL TYPE

#### Leonine ![unlocked](images/unlocked.gif)  Available!

The 'default' EQA tail. A long tailbone with any length hair growth from the end of the tail, like a lion's tail!
#### Donkey ![unlocked](images/unlocked.gif)  Available!

A short tailbone with any length hair growth from the tip of the tail. Like a donkey's!
Horse ![locked](images/locked.gif) You must first unlock this.

A short tailbone with long hair growth. Like a horse's!
Unicorn ![locked](images/locked.gif) You must first unlock this.

A long tailbone with any length hair growth all the way along the tail. Like a unicorn's!

### FACE TYPE

#### Dished ![unlocked](images/unlocked.gif) Available!

While the default EQA's face is only slightly dished, you can create one with a more pronounced dish if you would like!
#### Straight ![unlocked](images/unlocked.gif) Available!

Straight nose like thoroughbreds and the like.
#### Roman ![locked](images/locked.gif) You must first unlock this.

The roman nose can be subtle like some Iberian breeds, or more pronounced like the kladruber's.

#### BEARD

#### No beard ![unlocked](images/unlocked.gif)  Available!

A beardless EQA!
#### Beard ![unlocked](images/unlocked.gif)  Available!

You can facial hair to your EQA! It can be any texture or length. Unicorn/ goat's, long, curly, straight, mustache, you name it! The beard can appear on the lips, under the chin, and on the underside of the neck.

Different builds like baroque, wild, heavy draft, pony, and pygmy can be unlocked with special items! With special items your EQA may have horns and other mutations! Fangs, horns, and hooves must be made of bone or keratin by default, but can be made out of precious materials with special items.
Eyes can be any colour, and may have metallic or precious stone colouring without the use of any items! The mane and tail can grow to virtually any length and can be of any texture. The height of your character must correspond to their type!
#### WINGS & FEATHERS

Your character can have almost any colour found on real-life bird species, however, special colourings like iridescent and pearlescent feathers, and the colourings of precious metals and stones require special items!

#### WING TYPE

#### Long and Narrow ![unlocked](images/unlocked.gif)  Available!

Like seabirds, this wing type is built for endurance.
#### Short and Narrow ![unlocked](images/unlocked.gif) Available!

Like falcons and kites, this wing type is built for speed, especially around cliffs.
#### Long and Broad ![unlocked](images/unlocked.gif)  Available!

Like eagles and vultures, this wing type is built for soaring.
#### Short and Broad ![unlocked](images/unlocked.gif)  Available!

Like hawks and owls, this wing type is built for agility, especially through forests.
#### Crooked ![unlocked](images/unlocked.gif)  Available!

Like osprey, this wing type is built for coastal activities.
#### Hovering ![locked](images/locked.gif) You must first unlock this.

Like hummingbirds, this wing type is built for hovering stationary in the air.

#### DESIGN CHANGES

If and when you want to make a change to your character's design, you are free to do so. This includes updating your character if you want to apply a build, face, or tail type that you have unlocked.
