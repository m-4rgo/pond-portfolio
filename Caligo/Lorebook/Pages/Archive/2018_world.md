ABOUT THE WORLD

THE WORLD is much bigger than earth. It is the size of Jupiter; ten times bigger than our planet.
THE WORLD has two suns. One is a large red giant, and orbiting that giant is a smaller, yellower, and brighter little sun.
THE WORLD's moon is pale blue and has two ice white rings, which are arranged at an angle to one-another, so that they appear to cross over each other in an X shape.
Each season in THE WORLD lasts for one month months of our real time. This means that for every real-world year that elapses, three years elapse in THE WORLD.

KEY GAME PRINCIPLES

Trust. Trust eachother to play by the rules, because that makes the game more fun for everyone!

CLANS

This young world is unruly. It needs order! Is your character the one to lead change and create a community? In THE WORLD your character can make their own clan, or civilisation, and can invite others to join it. It is up to your character to define the systems that run their community, and the lands that they govern. Your character isn't a leader? They can join anyone else's clan. Just approach the character's owner for RP!

IDEOLOGIES

How was the world created? This land is a young one, but your character has heard rumours; snippets of information, about who was there and what they did. It's time for a new religion to be born, and the "truth" about the world's creation to be revealed! Any character can found their own religion based on their own concept of how the world was created and how Equus Aves came to be. It's completely up to your character to define their ideology and to enlist others for their cause. They can join anyone else's ideology. Just approach the character's owner for RP!

TRADE

Characters can trade items freely with one another! Just negotiate the trade in RP! Your character can also develop a trade as a canon way for you to sell accessory designs too if you like (e.g armour, jewellery, weapons, etc).

DAEMONS

Your character can have any daemon they like! The daemon should be a real-life, extant or extinct animal. They can have any colour that is found on their real-life counterpart, but may have small amounts of unnatural colouring too. Your character can meet their daemon at any time! It might be rewarding for you to create a piece of work about their union.
