Hello everyone! p-ond here! It's been a long time since I've been in touch and you might have noticed that our pause for the spring turned out to be a pause for the... Indefinite...

However! I've put a lot of thought into it and have decided to make Equus Aves an open species; free to create, breed, roleplay, etc etc etc. I still have a huge amount of pride in my creations and in my mind many many ideas for the species and their world, i just lack the time to make it all cohesive enough for everyone to understand at the moment. I do hope to return to the group and species and make it active once again in the future, with less of a focus on harpg, and more of a roleplay based function. In the meantime, everyone is free to do whatever they like with their EQAs; you may:
-Transfer your imports to another active group (provided you give the EquusAvis group/ import artist credit for the original design)
-Use your EQAs in any roleplay on or off deviant art. If you must choose a real world breed to suit your character, we recommend akhal teke, next Perce, or Turkoman breeds, however you're more than free to choose something else!
-Breed, roleplay, show, etc etc your EQAs anywhere and everywhere (provided credit is given to us for the original design)
-Edit your EQAs design/sex/ etc etc (again as long as credit is given to us for the original design and eqa traits etc etc)

We will absolutely accept any and all art featuring your Equus avis characters into the group and this place will remain a hub for anyone and everyone who takes an interest. I'll leave all the group resources up for you guys too.

If you have any other questions about things you might want to do with your EQAs then go ahead and ask us! But generally, you're free to do anything you want as long as the group &/or the original artist (p-ond or star-gutz) are given credit for the design and species.

Hugs and kisses and we wish you all so well! Until next time <3
