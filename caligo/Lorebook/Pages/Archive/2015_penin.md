Intro


This page displays the point system by which your Equus Avis can be ranked, or you can use to buy certain valuables from the Equus Avis shop.
You must count these points on the owner reference of your Equus Avis, under the title 'Penin:'. You must show evidence of every single point listed. You may link to a stashed version of another's work, granted you have their permission to stash the work and list it. Linking to stashed work of others acts as a precaution in the circumstance that they delete the piece from their account.

When applying for an Equus Avis rank or item from the shop, your EQA's penin points will be counted by an administrator, and your request will be declined if you have counted wrong and the points are insufficient, so please make sure to double check before you send in a request.

For an example of how to count penin, look to ID#0025 puymra. You may also create a journal for your EQA, specifically for its penin, breeding, and/or store purchases.

Official Reference:
An official Equus Avis reference (on any of the three sets of lines) will bag you 1 starting point, so every Equus Avis import will start off with a 1 point total. You will need to list the official reference of your Equus Avis on your owner reference.
Similarly, an accepted foal design gives 1 point.




Pedigree Penin:
For each ancestor in your EQA's pedigree, add 5 penin
For each ancestor in your EQA's pedigree with a coat mutation, add 7.5 penin


Offspring Penin:
For each offspring, add 2.5 penin
For each offspring with a coat mutation, add 5 penin




Standard art:
For each uncoloured headshot image, add 5 penin
For each uncoloured fullbody image, add 10 penin
For each coloured headshot image, add 10 penin
For each coloured fullbody image, add 20 penin


Chibi art:
For each uncoloured headshot image, add 2.5 penin
For each uncoloured fullbody image, add 5 penin
For each coloured headshot image, add  5 penin
For each coloured fullbody image, add 10 penin


Pixel art:
For each uncoloured headshot image, add 5 penin
For each uncoloured fullbody image, add 7 penin
For each coloured headshot image, add 10 penin
For each coloured fullbody image, add 14 penin


Backgrounds:
For a transparent or block/gradient colour background, or a background which is solely a pre-made pattern overlay, add 0 penin
For simple background outlines with few colours (eg, this image), add 3 penin
For simple original pattern backgrounds (eg, this image), add 5 penin
For complex original pattern backgrounds (eg, this image), add 9 penin
For medium detail backgrounds, (eg anything ranging from this image, to this, to this image), add 7 penin
For very detailed and realistic backgrounds, add 13 penin


Animation:
Find the category above that the art falls into and use the point number as a base. Half the base you've just found, and add it back to the base. For example, a base of 10 would be halved and added to become 15. Then, add the point number from this section to the new base to get the total for the piece.
-For minor animations (defined as: a blink, tail swish, ear flick, bounce (icons), etc), add 5 penin
(this category stacks, so if your piece includes more than one minor animation, add another 5 penin for every one. For example, if a piece had a blink and a tail swish, 10 penin would be added. If it had a blink, ear swish and tail flick, add 15 penin, etc.)
-For medium animations (defined as: a leg or head movement, a kick out, etc), add 10 penin
(this category stacks, so if your piece includes more than one medium animation, add another 10 penin for every one. For example, if a piece had a head movement and kick out, 20 penin would be added. If it had a head movement, kick out, and add 15 penin, etc.)
-For major animations (defined as: a rear, buck, walk trot canter or gallop cycle, etc), add 15 penin
(this category stacks, so if your piece includes more than one major animation, add another 15 penin for every one)
-For background animations (defined as: moving background characters or features, so birds, tree movements, clouds, etc), add 10 penin.
(this category this category stacks, so if your piece includes more than one background animation, add another 10 penin for every one)


Literature:
This category is for witten work. 'Art by others than the owner' and 'When drawing others' Equus Avis' rules apply here, so be sure to check those to add the available points.
For short descriptive works (fewer than approximately 650 words), add 9 penin
For long descriptive works (more than approximately 650 words), add 18 penin
For short poetic pieces, add 7 penin
For long poetic pieces, add 12 penin


Manipulations
For manipulated headshots, add 9 penin
for manipulated fullbodies, add 18 penin
For manipulated backgrounds (with either fullbody or headshot), add 12 penin




When colouring linearts that were NOT made by yourself
(this rule does not apply for own-made linearts and they may be counted as normal)
You will be awarded penin points for the 'colouring' portion of work only.
For standard headshot colour-ins, add 5 penin
For standard fullbody colour-ins, add 10 penin
For chibi headshot colour-ins, add 2.5 penin
For chibi fullbody colour-ins, add 5 penin
For pixel headshot colour-ins, add 5 penin
For pixel fullbody colour-ins, add 7 penin



Art by others than the owner
This category simply, is an additional for any art of your EQA that was not drawn by you. This can include gifts, commissions, and to an extent, collaborations.
Find the category above that the art falls into and use the point number as a base. Add the point number from this section to the base to get the total for the piece.
For commissioned work, add 5 penin
For gifted work, add 6 penin
For collaborated work, add 2 penin
Art trades are considered gifts.


When drawing others' Equus Avis
Total the points using the categories above for the art piece you made. When you have the total, half it, and add the halved points to the Equus Avis of your choice. You may not split the points between numerous EQAs, as it creates complications that are difficult to keep track of. If you do not yet own an Equus Avis, you may 'save' the points until you do own one. If you never own an Equus Avis or never wish to own one, the points become void; they cannot be used by anyone else nor by you on anyone else's EQAs.
