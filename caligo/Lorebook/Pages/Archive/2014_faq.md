Intro


Please read the guidelines below to help you decide whether an EQA is for you!


What can I do with my EQA?

You’re free to use your EQAs in an almost infinite number of ways! From roleplay, to harpg, to casual art making, we love to see all the creative ways the community interacts with these wonderful beasts! All we ask is that:

When using your EQA off-site (outside of DA) you credit the DA group for the species and design, and make sure to credit us when anyone compliments or asks about your character’s design!
You keep your EQA’s design as it is shown on its import reference EXCEPT when you have bought and applied a conco.
If you want to give your EQA magical powers for the sake of roleplay, the powers you give them must leave their physical form and design intact, as they are on their import reference, EXCEPT when you have bought and applied a conco.

If you’re not sure if your plan for your EQAs fits in with the group’s guidelines, please note us or comment here, we’ll let you know!

How do I become a member?

Anyone can watch the group, and you can become a member at any time to keep up with news! You must become a member before, or on registering your first EQA.


How do I get an EQA?

Equus Aves are a closed breed, which quite simply means you can’t create your own. Never fear though, there’s loads of ways you can get your very own from the group!

Purchase an import
Win or buy one that’s up for adoption
Breed one out of two other EQAs
Buy one from another user

What happens if I don't register my EQA?

Nothing really, you just don't get any of the benefits that you do when you do register your EQA! When you register it, you can breed it, buy tack and tuskur from the store, buy concos, earn rewards from the ranking system, and enter it in community run competitions and projects!


I no longer want my EQA... What can I do with it?

There's a few things you can do!
Return your EQA to the group. We can't offer any refunds, but we'll happily put your old EQA back up for adoption for free!
Sell/give your EQA to another user. See this journal for information on how to!
:thumb525136742:
Have your EQA become deceased. After it's deceased, it may no longer be used for breeding (we will not accept future breeding requests after the death date, but all requests sent in before the date are still valid), and cannot be sold. UNLESS proof is given of a slot sale before the death date, that had not been used before the death date. Remember to keep proof of your slot sales and purchases!
